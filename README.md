# ai-dest-eval
Evaluation of AI-enabled systematic review platforms

CAMARADES Edinburgh

We aim to evaluate three AI-enabled systematic review platforms by replicating three recently published systematic reviews using these platforms and comparing their performance at each review stage against the original published findings. Our protocol is deposited on OSF (10.17605/OSF.IO/8NF6H).

The systematic reviews are: 
SR1 CAMARADES review – non human SR in “Trace amine-associated receptor 1 (TAAR1) agonism for psychosis: a living systematic review and meta-analysis of human and non-human data” https://wellcomeopenresearch.org/articles/9-182 

SR2 JBI review - “Comparison of diagnostic accuracy of rapid antigen tests for COVID-19 compared to the viral genetic test in adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis. DOI: 10.11124/JBIES-23-00291 

SR3 Cochrane review - “Community care navigation intervention for people who are at risk of unplanned hospital presentations” doi: 10.1002/14651858.CD014713.pub2. 

The systematic review platforms evaluated are Elicit.AI, Nested Knowledge and Scispace.
